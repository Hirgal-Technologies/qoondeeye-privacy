1. Xogta aan arurinno

Waxaan haynaa oo keliya xogta Qoondeeye u baahan yahay si uu kaaga caawiyo la socodka lacagahaaga.

Xogta akoonkaaga

  • check_circle Magacaaga, cinwaanka iimaylka, iyo xogta xaqiijinta ee uu maamulo adeegga aad ku gasho Qoondeeye.

Xogta maaliyadeed

  • check_circle Lacagaha aad gacanta ku darto, lacagaha Qoondeeye ka diiwaangeliyo SMS-ka, qaybaha, qoraallada, miisaaniyadaha, haraaga, iyo warbixinnada.

Xogta moobeyl-kaaga

  • check_circle Nooca app-ka Qoondeeye ee aad isticmaalayso, nooca moobeyl-kaaga, iyo nooca nidaamka hawlgalka.

Ogeysiisyada app-ka (Push Notifications)

  • check_circle Haddii aad oggolaato ogeysiisyada, Qoondeeye wuxuu kuu soo diri karaa xusuusinno, sida marka rukumad (subscription) waqtigeedu soo dhowaado, iyo ogeysiisyo kale oo app-ka la xiriira. Moobeyl-kaaga wuxuu helaa aqoonsi gaar ah oo ogeysiisyada laguugu soo diro (push token), waxaana dirista ogeysiisyada loo adeegsan karaa Apple, Google, iyo/ama Expo. Ogeysiisyada uma isticmaalno xayeysiis.

Sawirrada aad geliso waxaa lagu kaydin karaa kaydka internet-ka (cloud), sida Supabase, si aad uga heli karto qalabka kale ee aad Qoondeeye ku isticmaasho.

2. Soo dejinta SMS-ka (Android)

Qoondeeye wuxuu SMS-ka u isticmaalaa inuu si toos ah u diiwaangeliyo lacagaha ku jira ogeysiisyada ay soo diraan adeegyada lacagaha mobilka, bangiyada, iyo shirkadaha isgaarsiinta ee app-ku aqoonsado.

Oggolaanshaha SMS-ka

Si Qoondeeye lacagaha uga diiwaangeliyo SMS-ka si toos ah, inta lagu jiro dejinta app-ka wuxuu Android ka codsadaa oggolaanshaha RECEIVE_SMS si uu u ogaado SMS-yada lacagaha la xiriira marka ay soo galaan. Ma codsanno oggolaansho aan ku akhrino SMS-yadii hore ee telefoonkaaga ku jiray, mana akhrinno, la soconno, ama kaydinno fariimahaaga gaarka ah.

SMS-yada Qoondeeye isticmaalo

Qoondeeye wuxuu isticmaalaa oo keliya SMS-yada otomaatiga ah ee uu u aqoonsado ogeysiis lacageed, sida ogeysiisyada lacagaha mobilka ama bangiyada. Fariimaha gaarka ah, koodhadhka xaqiijinta, iyo SMS-yada kale ee aan lacagaha la xiriirin app-ku wuu iska indhatiraa. Qoondeeye ma akhriyo mana baaro SMS-yadii hore ee ku jiray telefoonkaaga.

Sida Qoondeeye u isticmaalo xogta SMS-ka

Marka Qoondeeye aqoonsado SMS lacageed, wuxuu ka soo saaraa oo keliya faahfaahinta muhiimka ah, sida cadadka, haddii ay tahay dakhli, kharash, wareejin, ku-shubasho ama nooc kale oo app-ku aqoonsado, waqtiga, haraaga, lambarka tixraaca, iyo xogta diraha ama qaataha haddii ay fariinta ku jirto.

Adiga ayaa maamula

Waxaad adeeggan maamuli kartaa wakhti kasta. Waxaad soo dejinta SMS-ka ka damin kartaa setting-ka Qoondeeye, ama waxaad oggolaanshaha SMS-ka ka joojin kartaa dejimaha telefoonkaaga. Haddii aad damiso, Qoondeeye si toos ah ugama diiwaangelin doono lacagaha cusub ee SMS-ka ku yimaada.

3. Sida aan xogta u isticmaalno

Waxaan xogtaada u isticmaalnaa:

Si adeeggu u shaqeeyo

Si aan kuu siino la socodka lacagaha iyo warbixinnada, isla markaana xogtaadu uga wada muuqato qalabka aad Qoondeeye ku isticmaasho.

Hagaajinta app-ka & caawimaadda

Si aan u hagaajinno shaqada iyo xasilloonida app-ka, uguna caawinno marka aad nala soo xiriirto.

4. Kaydinta & isku-xiridda xogta

Xogtaada waxaa lagu kaydin karaa moobeyl-kaaga, waxaana sidoo kale lagu kaydin karaa adeegyada internet-ka sida Supabase si kayd loogu sameeyo oo aad uga heli karto qalab kale. Sawirrada rasiidhada iyo sawirka profile-ka ee aad doorato inaad geliso waxaa sidoo kale lagu kaydin karaa kaydka internet-ka (cloud) isla ujeeddadaas. Qoondeeye ma kaydiyo sanduuqaaga SMS-ka oo dhan. Marka xogta laga soo saaray SMS-ka ay sugayso in internet-ka lagu kaydiyo, waxa la hayaa oo keliya faahfaahinta lacagta; qoraalka SMS-ka oo dhan lama kaydiyo.

5. Wadaagista xogta & amniga

Ma iibinno xogtaada gaarka ah. Waxaan xog kooban la wadaagi karnaa oo keliya adeeg-bixiyeyaasha Qoondeeye u baahan yahay si uu u shaqeeyo, sida:

Adeegyada farsamada

Adeegyada lagu kaydiyo xogta iyo faylasha internet-ka, isku-xiridda xogta, iyo adeegyada gelitaanka ama xaqiijinta aqoonsiga, sida Google Sign-In marka la isticmaalo.

Hagaajinta & la socodka ciladaha

Haddii adeegyadaas la shido, warbixinnada ciladaha iyo xogta ku saabsan sida app-ka loo isticmaalo waxaa loo adeegsan karaa hagaajinta Qoondeeye. Waxaan qaadnaa tallaabooyin macquul ah si aan u ilaalinno xogtaada.

  • sms_off Dami soo dejinta SMS-ka wakhti kasta
  • notifications_off Dami ogeysiisyada push-ka ee dejimaha telefoonkaaga

7. Muddada aan xogta hayno

Waxaan haynaa xogta dhaqaale ee la isku miday inta akoonkaagu shaqaynayo. Qoraalka SMS-ka ee ku meel-gaadhka ah waxaa lagu hayn karaa qalabkaaga (tusaale queue ilaa la soo dejiyo, ama Needs Review illaa 14 maalmood). Qoraalka SMS-ka oo dhan looma raro cloud-ka.

Tirtiridda akoonka & xogta

Gudaha app-ka: Settings → Tirtir akoonkayga Qoondeeye (waxaa la soo diri doonaa cusboonaysiinta privacy/compliance). Waxay tirtirtaa Auth user-ka, diiwaannada dhaqaale ee shakhsi ahaaneed, sawirrada, push tokens, iyo kaydka qalabka. Waxaad codsan kartaa tirtirid xitaa haddii dalab bundle ah uu sugayo; fulinta way sii socon kartaa ka dib marka akoonkaaga laga gooyo. Diiwaannada lacag-bixinta oo la qarshiyey waxaa la hayn karaa illaa 7 sano; dalabaadka bundle-ka illaa 2 sano (telefoonnada waa la redact-gareeyaa).

Codsi web (Google Play): Email u dir qoondeeye.app@gmail.com cinwaan “Qoondeeye account deletion request” iyo email/telefoonka akoonka. Waanu ka shaqaynaynaa tirtiridda adigoon app-ka dib u dejin.

8. Isbeddellada lagu sameeyo siyaasaddan

Waxaan mararka qaarkood cusboonaysiin karnaa siyaasaddan. Marka aan wax ka beddelno, taariikhda “Markii ugu dambeysay la cusboonaysiiyay” ee kore ayaan cusboonaysiin doonaa.

2. Optional SMS import (Android & iOS)

Qoondeeye offers an optional SMS auto-import feature to help record transaction alerts sent by supported local mobile money services, banking institutions, and telecommunication providers.

Permissions

To capture transactions automatically, Qoondeeye requests the Android RECEIVE_SMS permission so it can detect incoming financial notifications. We do not request access to read your existing SMS inbox or history, and we do not monitor, read, or store personal conversations.

iOS (Shortcuts)

iOS cannot listen to SMS in the background the way Android can. When you set up the optional Shortcut, supported messages from sender 192 can be forwarded into Qoondeeye via an App Intent (“Process Financial SMS”) while the phone is locked. You control whether that Shortcut runs.

What messages are processed

The app processes only recognized, automated financial transaction receipts and mobile wallet notifications. All other personal messages, text verification codes, and unrelated SMS communications are completely ignored. Qoondeeye does not read or scan your historical SMS archive.

How SMS data is used

transaction details—such as transaction amounts, transaction type (income, expense, transfer, top-up, or other supported transaction types), timestamps, balances, reference IDs, and masked sender/recipient identifiers where available.

Your control

SMS import is entirely voluntary and remains disabled by default. You can revoke this permission or toggle the feature off within the application settings at any time.

4. Storage & sync

Your records may be stored on your device and synced with backend services (such as Supabase) for backup and multi-device access. Optional receipt images and profile photos may be stored in associated cloud file storage for the same purpose. We do not upload your full SMS inbox, and queued SMS imports store parsed transaction fields only, not your full SMS message body.

7. Data retention

We keep synced finance data while your account is active. Temporary on-device SMS bodies may be kept in a protected capture queue until processed, or in Needs Review for up to 14 days, then cleared. Raw SMS bodies are not uploaded to cloud sync.

Account & data deletion

In-app: Settings → Delete my Qoondeeye account (shipping with the privacy/compliance app update). This removes your Auth user, personal finance records, profile images/receipts where applicable, push tokens, and local caches. You can request deletion while a bundle order is pending; fulfillment may continue after your personal account is detached. Anonymized payment/subscription records may be retained up to 7 years for fraud prevention and accounting. Bundle order records may be retained up to 2 years for fulfillment and audit (phones redacted).

Web request (Google Play): Email qoondeeye.app@gmail.com with the subject “Qoondeeye account deletion request” and the email or phone used for the account. We process deletion without requiring you to reinstall the app.
